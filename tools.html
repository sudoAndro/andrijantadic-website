What every website
knows about you.

No login, no cookies, no tracking script. Just what your browser and your connection give away automatically, on every visit, to every website. Everything below is read live in your browser – nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Network

Your connection

This is what the server sees the moment you connect – before the page has even loaded.

Device

Your browser and device

Any website can read these values with a few lines of JavaScript. No permission prompt, no warning.

Fingerprint

Your browser fingerprint

Combine all the values above and run them through a hash function – the result is often unique enough to recognize you again on your next visit. Even without cookies, even in a private window.

···· ···· ···· ····

Reload the page: the fingerprint stays the same. Open it in another browser: it changes. Real trackers use even more signals (canvas, fonts, audio) – this is the simple version.

Protection

What you can do about it

You can't become invisible – but you can make yourself a lot harder to track.

🌍

Use a VPN

Hides your real IP address and location. But: your fingerprint stays the same – a VPN only protects the network layer.

🦊

Privacy browser

Firefox with strict tracking protection, Brave or the Tor Browser deliberately report generic values – so you look like many others.

🚫

Block trackers

uBlock Origin in the browser, Pi-hole for the whole network: if tracking scripts never load, they can't collect anything.

🔁

Stay aware

Come back here with your VPN on and off, or from your phone. Seeing the difference yourself is the best lesson.
